About
Level Up.

A digital invitation studio focused on turning ordinary event invitations into memorable experiences.

Why Level Up?

An invitation is often the first glimpse guests receive of an event. It should create excitement before the event even begins.

Level Up was created to bring a more modern and interactive approach to digital invitations — combining design, technology and personalization.

Instead of giving customers a complicated tool to build everything themselves, Level Up handles the design and creates the finished invitation personally.
